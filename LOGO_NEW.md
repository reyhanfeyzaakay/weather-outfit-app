# New OutfitPack Logo Design

The new OutfitPack logo designed on 2026-03-23 replaces the old WeatherFit logo with a fresh, modern aesthetic that represents our brand's commitment to style and functionality. It features vibrant colors and a sleek design that resonates with our target audience, embodying the spirit of outdoor fashion.

## Design Elements
- **Colors**: Bright and engaging color palette.
- **Typography**: Modern and clean fonts to improve readability.
- **Imagery**: Icons that reflect activity and adventure.

This new logo aims to enhance brand recognition and align with the lifestyle of our users.
